How to Use

• Select owner (White/Black/Erase) and piece type

• Click squares to place/remove pieces

• Right-click any square to clear it completely

• Click a piece to see only its attack range (blue highlight)

• Stack Mode: click occupied square to add piece on top (max 3)

• Erase + Stack Mode: removes only the top piece

• Show Attacks: highlights all squares attacked by the selected owner

• Level indicators: numbers in top-left show attacker's stack size

Colors: Blue = selected piece attacks | Red = all attacks | Numbers: 1 2 3
